Sam Spectral Control @VERSION@

A spectral and dynamic equalizer for mono, stereo, surround and immersive (up to 9.1.6) tracks.

This installer puts the plug-in into the system plug-in folders:
  VST3:        /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  Audio Unit:  /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components (when included)

"Customize" lets you choose the components. To remove the plug-in later, run
"Uninstall Sam Spectral Control.command" in /Library/Application Support/Sam Spectral Control.

Requires macOS 11 or later (Apple silicon or Intel).
